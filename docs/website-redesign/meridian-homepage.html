Questions owners ask

Before you book.

Is our data safe?

Everything runs in your accounts under your permissions. We use business-tier AI plans that don't train on your data, and the roadmap lists exactly which data goes where. Nothing runs on our servers without your say.

What happens when the AI gets it wrong?

Anything customer-facing gets a human check until you're comfortable removing it. Unusual cases route to a person instead of guessing. The 30 days of tuning after launch exist for exactly this.

Do we need someone technical on staff?

No. If your team can use email and a spreadsheet, they can use this. We train whoever will run it and leave written instructions.

Which tools do you work with?

Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Teams, HubSpot, Pipedrive, QuickBooks, Notion, Airtable, and most things with an inbox or an API. If you use something unusual, mention it on the fit call.

What does it cost to run afterwards?

Typically $50–300 a month in AI and automation subscriptions, in your name. The roadmap lists them before you commit to a build.

How long does it take?

Audit: two weeks. First workflow: three to four weeks after that. Most clients have something running inside six weeks of the fit call.

What if it doesn't work?

If the audit finds nothing worth building, we say so and you keep the roadmap. On a build, we keep tuning until it meets the spec we agreed together.

Do you work in French? Are you in Montréal?

Oui. Montréal-based, on-site across Greater Montréal, remote everywhere else in North America.
